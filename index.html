Frequently asked questions

Before we get started.

Does ED Music Growth guarantee streams or playlists?

No. We provide agreed strategy and support. Streaming, placements, audience growth and revenue depend on many factors and cannot be guaranteed.

Is ED Music Growth part of Ditto Music?

No. ED Music Growth is independent. Ditto Music is a separate third-party provider. Any use of its services is subject to its own terms and confirmed fees.

Can artists work with you from other countries?

Yes. We accept enquiries from artists worldwide. Availability, payment method, project scope and delivery dates are confirmed individually.

How do I book a service?

Send an enquiry through WhatsApp or email with your artist profile, release date and goals. We'll discuss the scope and confirm the terms before you pay.
